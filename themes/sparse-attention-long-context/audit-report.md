| claim:32 | mesurer-l-acceleration | established | corrected | 1 | 1 | 1 | 4 | soutien✓ réfutation✗~ | Because feedforward/projection FLOPs scale with model width while attention FLOPs scale quadratically with sequence length, attention's share of prefill compute grows with context length — for a ~175B-parameter model it goes from well under 1% of total FLOPs at a few hundred tokens to roughly 31% at 32K tokens (Casson); but this crossover is width-dependent (attention/total ≈ T/(8·d_model) per the scaling-book derivation), so narrower models reach the same share at shorter sequences (e.g. Gemma-27B, d_model=4,608, crosses ~50% around 37K tokens).
